I'm truly delighted anytime I discover some website I use has added support for [Passkeys](https://www.passkeys.com/what-are-passkeys.html). I was aware, however that moving them between devices or Password Managers could prove troublesome. I really enjoyed [Ricky Mondello's](https://rmondello.com/) piece a couple weeks back which got into some of the details behind the kinds of clever solutions being devised to build a more private and secure internet for all[^1]. Ricky is a very sharp engineer and a powerful communicator on the impacts and value of passkeys for regular people.

[^1]: Shamefully, it was only by reading this post that I learned that the company behind my password manager of choice 1Password has seen fit to [contribute financial support to Omarchy](https://www.alilleybrinker.com/blog/1password-funds-ethnic-cleansing/), the Linux distribution controlled by vocal neo-nazi DHH. Disgraceful. Switching Password Managers myself just became a lot more attractive.
